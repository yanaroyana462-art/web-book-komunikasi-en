When interacting in public spaces—from neighbors, acquaintances, market vendors, and fellow commuters to netizens on social media—we encounter a diverse mix of cultural backgrounds, daily habits, educational levels, and personalities.

Not everyone shares our perspective, nor was everyone raised with ideal communication etiquette. This is where our true patience is tested: will we adopt a *patronizing* attitude, feel self-righteous, and jump to judgment, or will we embrace others patiently and convey goodness in a manner that is clear and easily understood?

> <p class="arabic">ادْعُ إِلَىٰ سَبِيلِ رَبِّكَ بِالْحِكْمَةِ وَالْمَوْعِظَةِ الْحَسَنَةِ ۖ وَجَادِلْهُم بِالَّتِي هِيَ أَحْسَنُ</p>
>
> *"Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom and good instruction, and argue with them in a way that is best."*  
> — **QS. An-Nahl [16]: 125**

## 4 Principles of Public Communication Inspired by the Prophet ﷺ

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ never restricted kind and gentle speech to those closest to him. He consistently interacted with laypeople, strangers, and even those who had not yet sympathized with him, using the noble example of exemplary character.

### 1. Making Things Easy, Not Difficult (*Inclusivity*)
When communicating norms, guidance, or rules to the general public, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ always chose the simplest, friendliest, and most approachable method. He avoided using complex language or burdening his listeners.

> <p class="arabic">يَسِّرُوا وَلَا تُعَسِّرُوا وَبَشِّرُوا وَلَا تُنَفِّرُوا</p>
>
> *"Make things easy and do not make them difficult; give glad tidings and do not repel people."*  
> — **HR. Bukhari no. 69**

<div class="kisah-box">

> When Prophet Muhammad ﷺ dispatched Mu'adh bin Jabal and Abu Musa Al-Ash'ari radhiyallahu 'anhuma to Yemen to teach the local community, his primary instruction was not a list of threats or complex laws, but rather ease and a welcoming approach so that people felt comfortable.

</div>

**Daily Application:**
* **In Public Spaces:** Use clear, friendly, and straightforward directions when helping strangers ask for guidance or local rules.
* **Social Media Content:** Share ideas or information using accessible and inclusive language without confusing technical jargon.

---

### 2. Adapting Language to the Listener's Understanding
Speaking with an expert or academic naturally requires a different approach than conversing with laypeople. Prophet Muhammad ﷺ never displayed complex terminology in front of ordinary people just to sound intellectual.

> <p class="arabic">كَلِّمُوا النَّاسَ عَلَى قَدْرِ عُقُولِهِمْ</p>
>
> *"Speak to people according to their level of understanding."*  
> — **HR. Al-Dailami**

<div class="kisah-box">

> A Bedouin from the desert came and inquired about Islam in a very plain and direct manner. Prophet Muhammad ﷺ took no offense at his rustic style, nor did he offer complex theological explanations. 
> 
> He answered simply: *"Five daily prayers, fasting during Ramadan, and paying zakat."* When the Bedouin stated he would strictly adhere to these basic obligations and nothing more, the Prophet smiled and validated him without adding complicated conditions (HR. Bukhari no. 46).

</div>

**Daily Application:**
* **Public Education:** Explain technical subjects (such as programming, legal regulations, or finance) to beginners using simple, everyday analogies.
* **Empathetic Language:** Choose words that are easily understood by elders or children during conversations.

---

### 3. Responding to Ignorance with Education, Not Insults
Laypeople often make mistakes or violate norms not out of malice, but due to a lack of knowledge. Prophet Muhammad ﷺ always understood the root cause of ignorance and never responded to errors with anger or insult.

<div class="kisah-box">

> A young man once approached Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and requested publicly: *"O Messenger of Allah, grant me permission to commit adultery!"* Surrounding companions were shocked and prepared to reprimand him sternly. 
> 
> However, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ calmed his companions and invited the youth to sit close to him. He engaged him in an empathetic dialogue: *"Would you like that for your mother? Your daughter? Or your sister?"* 
> 
> The youth replied: *"No, by Allah."* Prophet Muhammad ﷺ then placed his hand gently on the young man's chest and prayed for him. The youth departed with a heart completely free of desire for wrongdoing (HR. Ahmad).

</div>

**Daily Application:**
* **Conflict Resolution:** When a commuter or customer makes a mistake in public, offer polite guidance without shouting or humiliating them.
* **Social Media Interactions:** Respond to mistaken comments with factual data and friendly explanations, avoiding *cyberbullying*.

---

### 4. Upholding Courtesy in Public Spaces & Social Media
In public domains, good speech and thoughtful typing are those that bring security and tranquility to the surrounding environment—not actions that incite, spread hoaxes, or provoke hostility.

> <p class="arabic">الْمُسْلِمُ مَنْ سَلِمَ الْمُسْلِمُونَ مِنْ لِسَانِهِ وَيَدِهِ</p>
>
> *"A true Muslim is one from whose tongue and hand others are safe."*  
> — **HR. Bukhari no. 10**

<div class="kisah-box">

> Prophet Muhammad ﷺ emphasized that a key sign of moral perfection is one's ability to refrain from harming, hurting, or intimidating others—whether through spoken words (*tongue*) or written actions (*hand*).

</div>

**Daily Application:**
* **Internet Etiquette (*Netiquette*):** Refuse to participate in hate speech (*hate speech*), verify hoaxes before sharing, and respect others' privacy.
* **Public Amenities:** Speak at a reasonable volume when using public transport or open spaces to maintain shared comfort.

## Simple Real-Life Practice

Here is a comparison of communication styles when interacting with the general public or online community:

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Correcting Someone in Public">
    **Less Ideal Way (Humiliating & Judgmental):**  
    "Hey! Have you no manners? Littering like that, did you never go to school?"

    ---

    **Prophetic Way (Educational & Courteous):**  
    "Excuse me, the trash bin is just over there. Let's keep this place clean together."
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Responding to Unpleasant Online Comments">
    **Less Ideal Way (Emotional & Argumentative):**  
    Replying with harsh words, trading insults in the comments section, and attacking the opponent's personality to win the argument.

    ---

    **Prophetic Way (Guarding Speech & Defusing Arguments):**  
    Providing polite clarification if necessary, or choosing to remain silent and pray for goodness if the interaction is purely provocative.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Daily Public Interaction Checklist

:::tip[Testing Your Verbal Empathy]
Before posting on social media or correcting a stranger in public, evaluate these 3 points:

1. **Intention:** Am I offering feedback to rectify a situation, or simply venting emotion to feel superior?
2. **Language:** Are my words respectful, considerate, and easy for the general public to understand?
3. **Impact:** Do my words or posts today contribute to harmony and peace in the surrounding environment?
:::

> **Closing Message:**  
> The maturity of one's character is clearly reflected in how one treats those from whom one expects nothing in return. Keep spreading soothing words wherever you go, for gracious speech is the most effective form of personal example.
