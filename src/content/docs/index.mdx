## 10 Core Chapters of Prophetic Communication

- **Foundations & Speech Styles:** Understanding speech ethics and the 6 *Qaulan* principles from the Qur'an.
- **The Art of Active Listening:** Emulating the total attentiveness and empathy of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ when listening.
- **Clarity & Non-Verbal Cues:** Paying attention to vocal intonation, articulation, and warm facial expressions.
- **Family Communication:** Cultivating speech warmth and gentleness with your spouse and children at home.
- **Professionalism & Work Teams:** Prioritizing mutual consultation, appreciating contributions, and safeguarding colleagues' dignity.
- **Public & Everyday Interactions:** Delivering simple, welcoming messages that offer reassurance in public spaces.
- **Handling Adversaries & Oppositions:** Maintaining speech control, diffusing insults, and praying for goodness amidst disagreements.
- **Leadership & Strategic Narrative:** Building inspiring visions, transparency, and leader composure.
- **Crisis & High Pressure:** Controlling emotions, easing panic, and maintaining speech firmness during critical moments.
- **Delivering Criticism & Educational Feedback:** Correcting mistakes with wisdom without wounding the listener's self-esteem.
- **References:** List of citations from Qur'anic verses and Hadith compilations.
