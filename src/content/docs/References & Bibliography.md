This digital book was compiled by referencing authentic sources to ensure that every principle conveyed rests upon a solid foundation. Below is the list of primary references utilized:

## Primary Sources

1. **The Noble Qur'an**: The primary source for the principles of *Qaulan* (speech and communication).
2. **Sahih al-Bukhari**: Authentic Hadith collection compiled by Imam al-Bukhari.
3. **Sahih Muslim**: Authentic Hadith collection compiled by Imam Muslim.
4. **Sunan Abi Dawud**: Hadith collection compiled by Imam Abu Dawud.
5. **Sunan at-Tirmidhi**: Hadith collection compiled by Imam at-Tirmidhi.
6. **Musnad Ahmad**: Hadith collection compiled by Imam Ahmad bin Hanbal.
7. **As-Sunan al-Kubra**: Hadith compilation by Imam Al-Baihaqi.

## Supporting Literature

* **As-Shama'il al-Muhammadiyah**: By Imam at-Tirmidhi (on the physical traits and noble character of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ).
* **Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum (The Sealed Nectar / Prophetic Biography)**: By Sheikh Safiur-Rahman al-Mubarakpuri.
* **Al-Adab al-Mufrad**: By Imam al-Bukhari (specifically addressing manners and ethics).
* **Riyadh as-Salihin (The Meadows of the Righteous)**: By Imam an-Nawawi (specifically chapters on guarding the tongue).
* **Ihya' 'Ulum ad-Din**: Specifically the chapter *Afat al-Lisan* (The Dangers of the Tongue) by Imam al-Ghazali.
